Meet the Smols

Minivoc's little siblings: about a third of his height, each with a prop, a personality and a very specific bubble tea order.

Smolvoc

the basic Smol

Likes everything, honestly. Specialty: the low five.

Strawberry milk with popping boba

Clipboard Smol

the planner

Keeps a list of everything and checks it twice.

Jasmine green milk tea, exactly 30% ice

Tie Smol

the professional

Very serious about business for someone this small.

Black milk tea, no sugar, no toppings

Hardhat Smol

the builder

Eager, loud and always fixing something.

Thai tea with double pearls

Chef Smol

the cook

A foodie who hums while he works and rates every cup out of 10.

Taro milk tea with coconut jelly

Specs Smol

the computer nerd

Precise, a little shy, stays up late debugging.

Matcha latte, light ice

The Twins

always together

The brave one (orange) goes first; the careful one (teal) covers his eyes, then claps. They go everywhere hand in hand.

Mango milk tea (brave) · plain milk tea (careful)
