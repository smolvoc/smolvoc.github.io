Idle Voc Tycoon

A tiny avocado. A very big dream.

A cozy idle tycoon. Pick a business (a bubble tea bar, a bakery, a flower shop or a toy store), give it a name, and watch tiny customers queue up in your living little shop. Upgrade, hire staff so it runs itself, and grow from a street cart into an HQ that fills the whole block.

  • Your business, your name on the sign
  • A shop that grows room by room
  • Keeps earning while you're away
